About the maths

Australian Curriculum v9.0, Year 2, Term 2 focus. Calm, self-paced, no timers. Built for a learner who needs more processing time: one step at a time, everything stays on screen, read-aloud, drag/tap answers, and tiered hints plus "Show me how" worked examples. Difficulty eases automatically if he's finding it tricky. Sources: ACARA, QCAA, Mathletics, UDL/CAST.
